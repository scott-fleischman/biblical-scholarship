# 09. Global and Critical Interpretation

## Why this file is needed

A bibliography can contain many high-quality commentaries and still look narrow if critical, global, and minoritized approaches appear only as an appendix. Specialists in feminist, womanist, Black, Latinx, Asian, African, Indigenous, queer, disability, ecological, postcolonial, decolonial, trauma, empire, class, and reception studies expect these approaches to be integrated book by book.

## Editorial principle

Do not create a “critical approaches” shelf that is disconnected from the biblical books. Use this file as both:

1. A methodological orientation.
2. A checklist for adding book-specific entries under Genesis, Exodus, Joshua, Prophets, Gospels, Paul, Revelation, and every other book.

## Feminist and gender-critical interpretation

### Core questions

- How do texts construct gender, kinship, reproduction, purity, marriage, motherhood, masculinity, and authority?
- Which women, gendered bodies, enslaved persons, foreigners, widows, and children are centered or erased?

## Integration checklist

For every biblical book file, add at least:

- One feminist/gender item.
- One critical race, womanist, Black, Latinx, African, Asian, Indigenous, or postcolonial item where available.
- One empire/coloniality item where relevant.
- One disability or body-focused item where relevant.
- One ecological/land/animal item where relevant.
- One trauma/memory item where relevant.
- One reception item outside elite academic commentary.
- One non-English scholarly tradition.
