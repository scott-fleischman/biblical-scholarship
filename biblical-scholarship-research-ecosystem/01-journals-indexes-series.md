# 01. Journals, Indexes, Series, and Article-Level Discovery

## Why this file matters

A bibliography becomes a research ecosystem only when it shows how scholarship is discovered, evaluated, and updated. Monographs and commentaries are necessary but insufficient. Specialists expect indexes, journals, review venues, monograph series, encyclopedia ecosystems, dissertation databases, manuscript catalogues, object databases, and conference programs.

## Core bibliographic indexes and discovery systems

### Biblical studies and theology

- **Atla Religion Database / ATLA RDB**: primary article and essay index for biblical studies, theology, church history, religion, and related fields.
- **Old Testament Abstracts**: core index for Hebrew Bible/Old Testament scholarship.
- **New Testament Abstracts**: core index for New Testament and early Christian scholarship.
- **Index Theologicus / IxTheo**: major open theological and religious-studies index, especially useful for German and European scholarship.
- **Index Religiosus**: bibliography for theology, religious studies, and church history, especially useful for European journals and essays.
- **Religious and Theological Abstracts**: useful for theology, reception, and adjacent religious-studies work.
- **Review of Biblical Literature**: essential review venue for recent books.
- **ZoteroBib / WorldCat / Library of Congress / Karlsruher Virtueller Katalog**: metadata verification and edition control.

### Classics, ancient history, and Greco-Roman religion

- **L'Année philologique**: indispensable for classics, ancient history, Greek/Latin literature, epigraphy, papyrology, philosophy, and Greco-Roman religion.
- **Gnomon Bibliographische Datenbank**: strong for classical philology and ancient history.
- **Dyabola / Zenon DAI**: archaeology, art history, and classical antiquity.
- **Oxford Bibliographies in Classics** and **Oxford Classical Dictionary**: orientation tools.
- **Brill's New Pauly**: standard classical reference work.
- **Perseus**, **TLG**, **PHI Latin Texts**, **PHI Greek Inscriptions**, **Packard Humanities Institute**: text and inscription discovery.

### Jewish studies, Hebrew, rabbinics, and Israel studies

- **RAMBI: Index of Articles on Jewish Studies**: core index for Jewish studies articles, including Hebrew, English, French, German, and other languages.
- **Index to Jewish Periodicals**: useful for modern Jewish reception, history, and culture.
- **Bar-Ilan Responsa Project**: rabbinic and legal corpora.
- **Sefaria**: broad open access Jewish textual library; useful for orientation and linking, not a replacement for critical editions.
- **Friedberg Jewish Manuscript Society / Friedberg Genizah Project**: manuscript and Geniza discovery.
- **Genizah Research Unit catalogues**: Cambridge Geniza manuscripts.
- **Ktiv: The International Collection of Digitized Hebrew Manuscripts**.
- **National Library of Israel catalogues**.
- **Historical Dictionary Project of the Academy of the Hebrew Language**.

### Dead Sea Scrolls, Second Temple Judaism, and ancient Judaism

- **Orion Center Bibliography**: essential for Dead Sea Scrolls and Second Temple Judaism.
- **Dead Sea Scrolls Electronic Library**.
- **DJD / Discoveries in the Judaean Desert** volumes.
- **Dead Sea Discoveries** journal bibliographies.
- **RAMBI** and **Atla** for broader early Judaism.
- **Pseudepigrapha and Apocrypha bibliographies** in Charlesworth, OTP, outside-canon handbook literature, and specialized journals.

### Archaeology, epigraphy, papyrology, numismatics, and material culture

- **ASOR / BASOR / Near Eastern Archaeology** resources.
- **Israel Antiquities Authority databases and reports**.
- **Hadashot Arkheologiyot / Excavations and Surveys in Israel**.
- **Israel Exploration Journal** and **Tel Aviv**.
- **Levant**, **Palestine Exploration Quarterly**, **Antiquity**, **Journal of Field Archaeology**.
- **Papyri.info**, **Duke Databank of Documentary Papyri**, **Heidelberger Gesamtverzeichnis**, **Trismegistos**.
- **Supplementum Epigraphicum Graecum**, **PHI Greek Inscriptions**, **EDH / EAGLE / EDCS** for Latin epigraphy.
- **CIIP: Corpus Inscriptionum Iudaeae/Palaestinae**.
- **IIP: Inscriptions of Israel/Palestine**.
- **KAI: Kanaanäische und Aramäische Inschriften**.
- **COS: Context of Scripture** and **RINAP / SAA / ORACC** for ancient Near Eastern texts.
- **RPC Online**, **ANS**, **Coin Hoards**, **Meshorer corpora**, and regional numismatic catalogues.

### Patristics, late antiquity, medieval studies, and reception

- **L'Année philologique** for patristic Greek/Latin and late antique contexts.
- **Bibliographia Patristica** and **L'Année philologique** for early Christian literature.
- **International Medieval Bibliography**.
- **Iter: Gateway to the Middle Ages and Renaissance**.
- **Feminae: Medieval Women and Gender Index**.
- **Patrologia Latina Database**, **Patrologia Graeca**, **CSEL**, **GCS**, **Corpus Christianorum**, **Sources Chrétiennes**, **CSCO**, **Patrologia Orientalis**.
- **BHL / BHG / BHO** for hagiography.
- **Pinakes** for Greek manuscripts.
- **HMML Reading Room** for manuscript discovery.
- **Dumbarton Oaks resources** for Byzantine and late antique reception.

### Islamic studies, Qur'an, Arabic Christianity, and late antique monotheism

- **Index Islamicus**.
- **Qur'anic Studies Online / Corpus Coranicum**.
- **Al-Tafsir.com** and other tafsir repositories, used critically with edition awareness.
- **Christian Arabic bibliography resources** and manuscript catalogues.
- **Syriaca.org** for Syriac persons, places, works, and manuscripts.
- **Gorgias Encyclopedic Dictionary of the Syriac Heritage**.
- **DASI / Semitic epigraphy resources** for Arabian and Semitic inscriptions.

### Music, art, literature, anthropology, and lived religion

- **RILM Abstracts of Music Literature** for biblical music, chant, oratorio, hymnody, gospel music, and spirituals.
- **Art Index / Art & Architecture Source / Index of Medieval Art** for visual reception and iconography.
- **MLA International Bibliography** for Bible and literature.
- **Anthropological Index Online / AnthroSource** for ethnographic Bible use and lived religion.
- **Sociological Abstracts** for Bible, politics, education, law, and public religion.
- **ERIC** for pedagogy and biblical literacy.

## Article-harvesting workflow by subfield

### Hebrew Bible / Old Testament

1. Start with Old Testament Abstracts, Atla, IxTheo, and RAMBI.
2. Search journal titles directly: `Vetus Testamentum`, `ZAW`, `JSOT`, `JBL`, `CBQ`, `Biblica`, `Revue Biblique`, `Hebrew Bible and Ancient Israel`, `Journal of Hebrew Scriptures`.
3. Add German and French terms: `Redaktionsgeschichte`, `Überlieferungsgeschichte`, `Literarkritik`, `Traditionsgeschichte`, `composition`, `formation`, `Fortschreibung`, `réécriture`, `rédaction`.
4. Add evidence-type terms: `epigraphy`, `ostraca`, `seals`, `bullae`, `radiocarbon`, `ceramic`, `survey`, `settlement`, `onomastics`, `cult`, `household`.
5. Add critical-method terms: `gender`, `feminist`, `womanist`, `disability`, `ecological`, `postcolonial`, `empire`, `trauma`, `race`, `class`.

### New Testament

1. Start with New Testament Abstracts, Atla, IxTheo, L'Année philologique, and RAMBI.
2. Search `NTS`, `NovT`, `JSNT`, `ZNW`, `JBL`, `CBQ`, `Early Christianity`, `JECS`, `JSPL`, `JSHJ`.
3. Pair biblical books with debate terms: `Synoptic Problem`, `Q`, `Farrer`, `Two Gospel`, `memory`, `orality`, `performance`, `empire`, `imperial cult`, `Paul within Judaism`, `apocalyptic Paul`, `pistis Christou`, `participation`, `slavery`, `household`, `ethnicity`, `anti-Judaism`.
4. Add papyrology and epigraphy for historical context: `association`, `benefaction`, `patronage`, `household`, `assembly`, `synagogue`, `diaspora`, `citizenship`, `taxation`, `manumission`.

### Rabbinics

1. Start with RAMBI, Index to Jewish Periodicals, Atla, JSTOR, IxTheo, and Hebrew-language databases.
2. Search by corpus: `Mishnah`, `Tosefta`, `Yerushalmi`, `Bavli`, `Sifra`, `Sifre`, `Mekhilta`, `Genesis Rabbah`, `Leviticus Rabbah`, `Tanhuma`, `Pesiqta`, `Midrash Tehillim`, `Targum`, `piyyut`, `Geniza`.
3. Search by method: `redaction`, `orality`, `scholasticism`, `attribution`, `legal fiction`, `anonymous layers`, `intertextuality`, `text reuse`, `manuscripts`, `paratext`, `gender`, `body`, `ritual`, `magic`.

### Qumran and Second Temple Judaism

1. Start with Orion, Dead Sea Discoveries, Revue de Qumrân, STDJ, JSJ, JJS, RAMBI, Atla.
2. Search by manuscript identifiers and genre: `1QS`, `CD`, `4QMMT`, `Hodayot`, `pesharim`, `War Scroll`, `Temple Scroll`, `Jubilees`, `Enoch`, `Aramaic Levi`, `Tobit`, `Ben Sira`.
3. Add debates: `sectarianism`, `halakhah`, `calendar`, `authority`, `rewritten scripture`, `sapiential`, `apocalyptic`, `purity`, `gender`, `paleography`, `radiocarbon`, `provenance`, `forgery`.

### Reception history

1. Search by medium, not only by biblical book: `homily`, `sermon`, `lectionary`, `iconography`, `hymn`, `chant`, `oratorio`, `children's Bible`, `film`, `comic`, `museum`, `app`, `meme`, `translation`, `missionary`, `school`, `law`, `politics`.
2. Search major reception reference works: `Encyclopedia of the Bible and Its Reception`, `Blackwell Bible Commentaries`, `Oxford Handbook` volumes, `Cambridge Companion` volumes.
3. Add regional terms: Africa, African American, Latin America, Caribbean, Indigenous, Pacific, Korean, Chinese, Japanese, Indian, Filipino, Arab, Palestinian, Jewish, Muslim, Eastern Orthodox, Ethiopian, Syriac, Coptic.

## Review venues and quality control

Use reviews to prevent stale bibliographies. Important review venues include:

- Review of Biblical Literature.
- Journal of Biblical Literature book reviews.
- Catholic Biblical Quarterly book reviews.
- Journal for the Study of Judaism reviews.
- Dead Sea Discoveries reviews.
- AJS Review.
- Vigiliae Christianae reviews.
- Journal of Early Christian Studies reviews.
- Bryn Mawr Classical Review.
- Reviews in Religion & Theology.
- Theologische Literaturzeitung.
- Religious Studies Review.
- H-Net reviews.

A book should not be listed as simply “important” if major reviews identify serious flaws. A file should record: positive reception, critique, whether the work is superseded, whether its data remain useful, and whether its framing is now considered dated.
