# 11. Early Christianity, Christian Apocrypha, and Patristic Exegesis

## Why this file is needed

A bibliography centered on the 27-book New Testament can miss the wider Christian-origins ecosystem: Apostolic Fathers, apocryphal gospels and acts, Nag Hammadi, martyrdom, early liturgy, heresiology, patristic exegesis, Syriac/Coptic/Armenian/Ethiopic traditions, and material Christian practice. Specialists expect Christian origins to extend beyond canonical boundaries.

## Scope

- Apostolic Fathers.
- Christian apocrypha.
- Nag Hammadi and related Coptic literature.
- Marcion, Valentinianism, “gnostic” debates, Montanism, heresiology.
- Martyrdom and persecution literature.
- Early Christian liturgy, baptism, Eucharist, prayer, hymnody.
- Patristic biblical exegesis.
- Early Christian material culture.
- Syriac, Coptic, Latin, Greek, Armenian, Georgian, Ethiopic, and Arabic Christian traditions.
- Jewish-Christian relations and anti-Judaism.

## Article-level debate clusters

### Apostolic Fathers and canon-adjacent Christianity

**Questions:** How do Apostolic Fathers use Scripture? What do they reveal about authority, community order, martyrdom, Eucharist, baptism, and Jewish-Christian relations?

**Search terms:** `Apostolic Fathers scripture`, `Didache Matthew`, `Ignatius Gospel`, `1 Clement scripture`, `Shepherd of Hermas canon`.

**Expected venues:** Vigiliae Christianae, JECS, Early Christianity, JTS, NTS, NovT, Studia Patristica.

### Gospel of Thomas and sayings tradition

**Questions:** Is Thomas independent, dependent on Synoptic tradition, or a mixture? How does Thomas affect historical Jesus research and early Christian diversity?

**Search terms:** `Gospel of Thomas Synoptic`, `Thomas Q`, `Thomas independent tradition`, `Thomas and Jesus research`, `Thomas Coptic Greek`.

**Expected scholars:** Stephen Patterson, April DeConick, Simon Gathercole, Mark Goodacre, Nicholas Perrin, Elaine Pagels, Marvin Meyer, Ismo Dunderberg.

### Gospel of Mary, Mary Magdalene, and gendered authority

**Questions:** How do apocryphal texts construct Mary Magdalene, revelation, apostolic authority, gender conflict, and knowledge?

**Search terms:** `Gospel of Mary`, `Mary Magdalene authority`, `apocryphal Mary`, `gender early Christianity`.

**Expected scholars:** Karen King, Ann Graham Brock, Deirdre Good, Jane Schaberg, Antti Marjanen, Nicola Denzey Lewis.

### Marcion and Gospel/textual history

**Questions:** How should Marcion’s Gospel and Apostolikon be reconstructed? What is their relation to Luke and Pauline reception?

**Search terms:** `Marcion Gospel Luke`, `Marcion Apostolikon`, `Marcion textual criticism`, `Marcion canon`.

**Expected scholars:** Harnack, Lieu, BeDuhn, Roth, Moll, Vinzent, Klinghardt, Judith Hartenstein.

### “Gnosticism” as category

**Questions:** Is “Gnosticism” a useful category? Should scholarship use more precise labels: Valentinian, Sethian, Thomasine, Nag Hammadi, Coptic Christian Platonist?

**Search terms:** `gnosticism category`, `Williams Rethinking Gnosticism`, `King What is Gnosticism`, `Sethian Valentinian`.

**Expected scholars:** Michael Williams, Karen King, David Brakke, Bentley Layton, Elaine Pagels, Einar Thomassen, Ismo Dunderberg.

### Martyrdom and persecution

**Questions:** How widespread was persecution? How did martyr texts construct body, gender, witness, empire, and identity?

**Search terms:** `early Christian martyrdom`, `persecution myth`, `Perpetua gender`, `Martyrdom Polycarp`, `martyr acts authenticity`.

**Expected scholars:** Frend, de Ste. Croix, Castelli, Perkins, Cobb, Middleton, Moss, Rebillard.

### Patristic exegesis and anti-Judaism

**Questions:** How do patristic interpreters read Hebrew Bible/Old Testament, Gospels, Paul, and Revelation? How do allegory, typology, rule of faith, and anti-Judaism interact?

**Search terms:** `patristic exegesis anti-Judaism`, `Origen exegesis`, `Augustine Jews scripture`, `Chrysostom Jews`, `Jerome Hebrew truth`.

**Expected scholars:** de Lubac, Simonetti, Young, Clark, Wilken, Fredriksen, Cohen, Schäfer, Lieu, Reinhartz, Paula Fredriksen, Jeremy Cohen.

### Early Christian liturgy and Scripture

**Questions:** How did biblical texts function in baptism, Eucharist, daily prayer, lectionaries, hymnody, catechesis, and pilgrimage?

**Search terms:** `early Christian liturgy scripture`, `lectionary Bible late antiquity`, `baptismal catechesis scripture`, `Egeria lectionary`.

**Expected scholars:** Bradshaw, Johnson, Taft, McGowan, Winkler, Day, Stewart, Harvey.

## Quality-control warnings

1. **Do not equate noncanonical with late, marginal, or worthless.** Each text requires its own date, manuscript history, and social context.
2. **Do not use “gnostic” as a catch-all.** Specify corpus and school when possible.
3. **Do not cite heresiologists as neutral reporters.** Irenaeus, Tertullian, Epiphanius, and others are polemical witnesses.
4. **Do not treat patristic exegesis only as theology.** It is also textual evidence, reception, rhetoric, pedagogy, liturgy, and social boundary-making.
5. **Do not collapse Greek, Latin, Syriac, Coptic, Armenian, Georgian, Ethiopic, and Arabic Christianities into one patristic tradition.**
6. **Flag anti-Judaism and supersessionism explicitly.**
7. **Use manuscript and versional controls for Christian apocrypha.** Titles often mask complex textual plurality.
