# 07. Primary Corpora and Critical Editions

## Why this file is needed

Specialists expect a bibliography to distinguish primary evidence from secondary interpretation. This file supplies a control layer for literary texts, inscriptions, papyri, manuscripts, archaeological reports, legal corpora, iconographic materials, and digital datasets used in biblical scholarship.

## Minimum evidence-control entry

Every primary corpus entry should specify:

- Corpus name.
- Language(s).
- Period.
- Critical edition(s).
- Major manuscript witnesses.
- Digital access points.
- Major lexica/grammars.
- Main scholarly debates.
- Related biblical books/topics.
- Reliability and provenance warnings.
