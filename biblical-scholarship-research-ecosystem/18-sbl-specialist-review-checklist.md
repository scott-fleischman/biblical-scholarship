# 18. SBL Specialist Review Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist simulates what a broad SBL review panel would ask when evaluating whether the bibliography has become a research ecosystem. It is designed for maintainers and outside reviewers.

## Global questions

- Can a user find articles, not only books?
- Can a user find primary evidence and critical editions?
- Can a user identify which journals and indexes keep the topic current?
- Can a user see live debates and not only settled conclusions?
- Can a user tell which works are classic, current, superseded, or contested?
- Can a user see non-English scholarship?
- Can a user see global and minoritized interpretation integrated into biblical books?
- Can a user see material culture and digital tools?
- Can a user see ethics and provenance warnings?
- Can a user see reception, liturgy, preaching, and lived religion?

## Hebrew Bible reviewer

- Are BHQ/BHS/HUBP and textual witnesses represented by book?
- Are LXX, DSS, SP, Targum, Peshitta, Vulgate, and other versions included where relevant?
- Are German, French, Hebrew, and Israeli debates represented?
- Are source/redaction/tradition-history debates mapped at article level?
- Are archaeology, epigraphy, and ANE corpora integrated for historical claims?
- Are feminist, womanist, Indigenous, disability, ecological, trauma, empire, and postcolonial approaches integrated book by book?
- Are reception and liturgical uses represented beyond Christian commentary?

## Pentateuch reviewer

- Does the file distinguish Documentary, Neo-Documentary, supplementary, fragmentary, and European redaction models?
- Are P, H, D, non-P, Deuteronomy, and legal revision debates represented?
- Are Samaritan Pentateuch and Qumran Pentateuch manuscripts included?
- Are ANE law, ritual, treaty, and scribal comparanda included?
- Are current German/French/Israeli debates included?

## Prophets reviewer

- Are Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, and the Twelve treated as compositional and textual problems?
- Are Jeremiah MT/LXX and Qumran witnesses included?
- Is empire treated materially and historically, not only theologically?
- Are trauma, gendered city metaphors, and sexualized prophetic rhetoric addressed?
- Is prophetic reception in liturgy, politics, music, and social justice included?

## Psalms/Wisdom reviewer

- Are form criticism, canonical shaping, cult, temple, synagogue, and church liturgy all represented?
- Are Hebrew poetry, parallelism, and performance included?
- Are piyyut, chant, hymnody, and musical reception included?
- Are Job/disability, Ecclesiastes/philosophy, Proverbs/gender, Song/sexuality, Lamentations/trauma included?

## New Testament Gospels reviewer

- Is there a dedicated Historical Jesus section?
- Is there a dedicated Synoptic Problem section with Q, Farrer, Griesbach/Two-Gospel, minor agreements, and Marcion debates?
- Are memory, orality, performance, and book culture represented?
- Are Jewish context and anti-Judaism handled explicitly?
- Are Galilean and Judean archaeology included?
- Are Gospel reception, Passion reception, film, liturgy, and preaching included?

## Pauline reviewer

- Are New Perspective, Paul within Judaism, apocalyptic Paul, participation, grace/gift, and empire included?
- Are race/ethnicity, gender, sexuality, slavery, economics, and disability included?
- Are inscriptions, papyri, associations, household, patronage, and city archaeology included?
- Are Reformation and modern political receptions included?
- Are Jewish scholars and non-supersessionist approaches represented?

## Textual critic

- Are major editions and projects listed with status?
- Are manuscripts, versions, lectionaries, patristic citations, and paratexts represented?
- Are book-level textual histories included?
- Are CBGM/ECM, BHQ/HUBP, Göttingen, Vetus Latina, Peshitta, Targumim, Coptic, Armenian, Georgian, Ethiopic, Arabic, Slavonic represented?
- Are “original text” assumptions defined?

## Septuagint scholar

- Are Göttingen, Rahlfs-Hanhart, NETS, Septuaginta Deutsch, La Bible d'Alexandrie included?
- Are translation technique, interlinear paradigm, recensions, Hexapla, kaige, Aquila, Symmachus, Theodotion represented?
- Is the bibliography book-specific rather than generic?
- Are Greek linguistics and Hellenistic Jewish contexts included?

## Qumran / Second Temple reviewer

- Are DJD, Orion, STDJ, Dead Sea Discoveries, Revue de Qumran, Meghillot included?
- Are biblical, sectarian, legal, sapiential, liturgical, apocalyptic, calendrical, and Aramaic texts represented?
- Are Qumran archaeology and provenance/forgery debates included?
- Are Philo, Josephus, Pseudepigrapha, diaspora, and material culture integrated?

## Rabbinics reviewer

- Is rabbinics treated independently, not merely as reception?
- Are Mishnah, Tosefta, Yerushalmi, Bavli, halakhic midrash, aggadic midrash, targum, piyyut, Geniza, Hekhalot, magic included?
- Are Hebrew-language scholarship and manuscript issues represented?
- Are redaction, orality, scholasticism, attribution, and manuscript variation treated methodologically?
- Are tannaitic, amoraic, Palestinian, Babylonian, and later layers separated?

## Archaeologist / epigrapher

- Are final excavation reports privileged over popular summaries?
- Are inscriptions, papyri, coins, seals, bullae, ostraca, and images included?
- Are provenance and forgery risks explicit?
- Are radiocarbon, ceramics, archaeobotany, zooarchaeology, GIS, and survey represented where relevant?
- Are colonial/nationalist archaeology histories flagged?

## Early Christianity / patristics reviewer

- Are Apostolic Fathers, Christian apocrypha, Nag Hammadi, Marcion, heresiology, martyrdom, liturgy, patristic exegesis, and material culture represented?
- Are Greek, Latin, Syriac, Coptic, Armenian, Georgian, Ethiopic, and Arabic traditions included?
- Are anti-Judaism and supersessionism flagged?
- Are noncanonical texts handled with manuscript and versional controls?

## Islam / Qur'an reviewer

- Are Qur'an, tafsir, isra'iliyyat, tahrif, Syriac Christianity, Christian Arabic, Jewish Arabic, and polemic included?
- Are late antique scriptural-discourse models represented?
- Are Arabic and non-Anglophone scholars included?
- Are biblical figures in Qur'an and tafsir linked book by book?

## Reception / lived religion reviewer

- Are art, music, preaching, liturgy, children’s Bibles, film, politics, law, digital media, and ethnography included?
- Are primary reception artifacts listed?
- Are Jewish, Christian, Islamic, secular, global, and popular receptions represented?
- Is harmful reception named?

## Critical/global reviewer

- Are feminist, womanist, Black, Latinx, Asian, African, Indigenous, queer, disability, ecological, trauma, class, empire, and postcolonial approaches integrated into book files?
- Are minoritized scholars represented as core scholars, not special-interest add-ons?
- Are colonial and racial histories of interpretation explicit?

## Final reviewer verdict categories

Use these categories for each file:

- `ecosystem-ready`: primary evidence, article level, journals/indexes, global/critical, reception, ethics, and maintenance all present.
- `strong scaffold`: good book/commentary coverage but missing one or two ecosystem layers.
- `reading-list only`: mostly books/commentaries, weak article and primary-source infrastructure.
- `field gap`: major subfield absent or seriously underbuilt.
- `specialist review required`: likely errors or omissions outside maintainer expertise.
