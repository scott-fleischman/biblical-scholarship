# 03. Book-by-Book Article Matrix

## Purpose

This file adds the article-level layer that commentary-oriented book lists usually lack. It does not replace commentaries; it tells the maintainer what article clusters, special issues, primary corpora, and critical approaches need to be represented under each book.

## Cross-book article clusters that should be explicit

### Hebrew Bible / Old Testament

- **Source/redaction/composition:** Genesis-Deuteronomy, Former Prophets, prophetic books, Psalms, wisdom, Chronicles-Ezra-Nehemiah.
- **Textual pluriformity:** MT/LXX/SP/DSS for Pentateuch, Samuel-Kings, Jeremiah, Psalms, Daniel, Esther.
- **Empire:** Egypt, Assyria, Babylon, Persia, Hellenistic empires, Rome.
- **Law and legal revision:** Covenant Code, Deuteronomy, H, P, Temple Scroll, Qumran, rabbinic reuse.
- **Gender and sexual violence:** Genesis, Exodus, Judges, Samuel, Prophets, Song, Esther, Daniel, Deuterocanon.
- **Trauma and disaster:** Exodus, conquest, exile, Jeremiah, Lamentations, Ezekiel, Psalms, Daniel, apocalyptic.
- **Ecology and land:** Genesis, Leviticus, Deuteronomy, Prophets, Psalms, Job, wisdom.

### New Testament

- **Synoptic Problem and Gospel sources:** Matthew, Mark, Luke, Q, Farrer, oral tradition, memory.
- **Anti-Judaism and Jewish-Christian relations:** Matthew, John, Acts, Paul, Hebrews, Revelation.
- **Empire and imperial cult:** Gospels, Acts, Paul, 1 Peter, Revelation.
- **Slavery and household:** Paulines, Deutero-Paulines, Pastorals, 1 Peter, Philemon.
- **Gender and sexuality:** Gospels, Paul, Pastorals, Revelation.
- **Material context:** inscriptions, papyri, associations, synagogues, households, cities, roads, coins.
- **Reception:** Reformation Paul, Johannine anti-Judaism, Revelation apocalyptic politics, Passion narratives, Eucharist, Pentecost.
