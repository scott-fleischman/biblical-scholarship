# Source Notes for Current-Article Anchors

This package distinguishes older canonical scholarship from recent article anchors. Older entries are included as scholarly waypoints and should be page-verified before conversion into a formal style sheet. Recent anchors were selected from current or near-current journal tables of contents and publisher pages consulted on 2026-04-19.

## Verification tiers

- `web-verified`: title and current-journal context were checked against a publisher, journal, society, or hosted TOC page during the construction of this expansion.
- `needs-page-verification`: the item is a standard scholarly waypoint, but precise page numbers, series data, edition/translation details, or original-publication venue should be verified in a library database before import into a formal bibliography.
- `recent-literature seed`: a contemporary item surfaced as a plausible current debate node; verify bibliographic details before formal citation.

## Warning

The graph is designed for research triage. It does not claim that every edge is the only or even primary line of dependence. The `confidence` field tells maintainers where to prioritize verification.
