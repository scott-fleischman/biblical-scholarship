# Appendix: Current-Issue Watch Protocol

Run this monthly.

1. Check current issues and FirstView/OnlineFirst for:
   - JBL
   - JSOT
   - JSNT
   - NTS
   - Novum Testamentum
   - CBQ
   - VT
   - DSD
   - JSJ
   - JSHJ
   - Biblical Interpretation
   - AJS Review
   - JIQSA
2. Add every article that appears to be:
   - an explicit review essay,
   - a direct response,
   - a symposium contribution,
   - a state-of-the-question article,
   - a method intervention,
   - a hot-topic article in a special issue.
3. For each article, record:
   - its first footnote,
   - the first five cited works,
   - all works named in the abstract,
   - all works named in section headings,
   - all works criticized in the conclusion.
4. Add at least three edges before marking it integrated.
