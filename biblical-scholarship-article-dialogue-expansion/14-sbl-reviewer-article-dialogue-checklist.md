# 14. SBL Reviewer Article-Dialogue Checklist

Use this checklist when asking specialists to review the bibliography.

## Universal questions

- Which article is the current live entry point for your subfield?
- What is the most important article it responds to?
- What older article or monograph is silently assumed?
- What article most sharply disagrees?
- What review essay or state-of-the-question article should be added?
- What non-English article is indispensable?
- Which works are now superseded?
- Which debate has shifted because of manuscript, archaeological, inscriptional, digital, or ethical evidence?
- What primary-source corpus must be cited before this debate can be responsibly discussed?

## Specialist prompts

### Hebrew Bible / Pentateuch
- Does the map represent both neo-documentary and European composition-history lines?
- Are P, non-P, D, H, and post-P debates article-level enough?
- Are legal-hermeneutical debates linked to ANE law and inner-biblical exegesis?

### Prophets
- Are textual-edition debates connected to redactional debates?
- Are trauma, empire, animals, ecology, and gender represented as core scholarship?

### Historical Jesus / Gospels
- Are criteria, memory, social history, class, empire, and decolonial historiography connected?
- Are Q/Farrer/Two-Gospel debates represented through direct article exchanges?

### Paul
- Does the map distinguish New Perspective, radical New Perspective, Paul within Judaism, apocalyptic Paul, gift theory, and social history?
- Are slavery, gender, ethnicity, and empire integrated?

### Textual criticism
- Are original-text, living-text, CBGM, palaeography, versions, and provenance connected?
- Is the DSS forgery debate built into evidence evaluation?

### Rabbinics
- Is rabbinics treated as a self-standing field?
- Are Tannaitic, Amoraic, Yerushalmi, Bavli, midrash, law, narrative, and material/lived religion all represented?

### Critical / global
- Are feminist, womanist, Black, postcolonial, decolonial, disability, ecological, animal, queer, Indigenous, and global translation approaches inside book-level debates?

### Reception
- Are art, music, liturgy, preaching, pedagogy, popular media, and digital reception included?
