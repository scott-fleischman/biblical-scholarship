# 13. Harvesting and Maintenance Playbook

## Article-dialogue harvesting protocol

For every topic, run three kinds of searches:

1. **Current issue search**  
   Search the last 24 months in relevant journals.
2. **Backward citation search**  
   Open the newest article and record every older article it explicitly names as an interlocutor.
3. **Forward citation search**  
   Use Google Scholar, Atla, Crossref, Scopus/Web of Science where available, and publisher pages to find who cites the waypoint.

## Required fields for each new node

- author
- year
- title
- article/book/review/symposium status
- journal/volume/issue/pages or book publisher
- subfield
- topic
- role in dialogue
- verification status
- notes

## Required fields for each edge

- source node
- target node
- relation
- topic
- claim delta
- confidence

## Confidence scale

- `high`: the later work directly names/responds to the earlier work or belongs to an explicit symposium/review exchange.
- `medium`: the relation is widely recognized or methodologically clear, but not necessarily a direct response.
- `low`: plausible current seed; verify before treating as a firm dialogue edge.
