Below is an **expansion layer** to append to the earlier biblical-studies / archaeology list. I kept the same checklist format, with **lectures and Great Courses first**, then **free university lecture series / lecture-like podcasts**, then **secondary books and audiobooks** for a wider knowledge base.

**Priority key:**
**P1** = strongest core addition
**P2** = strong next layer
**P3** = useful specialist / tangential background
**P4** = optional, caveat, or lower fit

I used The Great Courses / Wondrium catalog pages, Audible series pages, Open Yale, MIT OCW, Gresham College, Harvard Justice, and a few lecture-like academic podcast sources as the backbone. Great Courses currently lists broad history, business/finance, philosophy/religion, literature, science, and mathematics catalogs; Yale Open Courses provides full lectures with audio-only versions and transcripts; MIT OCW provides free notes, exams, and videos; and Gresham College hosts thousands of free public lecture videos. ([The Great Courses Shop][1])
