## Named-saint Audible / audiobook / lecture pass

**Priority key:**
**P1** = best historical-critical option, even when print-only
**P2** = useful serious audio, lecture, primary source, or sympathetic biography
**P3** = decent supplement but not strongly critical
**P4** = devotional, children’s, miracle-forward, Virtual Voice, or very thin historically

---

# 1. Best additions for your list

## Saint Helena

* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Julia Hillner — *Helena Augusta: Mother of the Empire*** — best critical historical option I found for Helena; not an Audible title in this pass. It treats Helena in Constantinian dynastic, imperial, gender, and political context rather than as simple legend. — [Barnes & Noble](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/helena-augusta-julia-hillner/1141544078)
* [ ] **P3 / Audible** — **Steve Castlen — *St. Helena: The Most Significant Woman in Human History*** — closest Audible biography, but the description frames it as a blend of historical fact and imaginative storytelling, so it is not ideal for strict historical-critical purposes. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/St-Helena-The-Most-Significant-Woman-in-Human-History-Audiobook/B0GV1ZGXCK)
* [ ] **P3 / Audible podcast** — **“St. Helena and the Finding of the True Cross”** — short lecture/podcast-style supplement, useful only as a quick overview. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/podcast/St-Helena-and-the-Finding-of-the-True-Cross-of-Christ/B0FMRNSYVD)

## Saint Clare of Assisi

* [ ] **P1 / Audible lecture** — **Joan Mueller — *Clare of Assisi: Her Life and Theology*** — still the best Audible option for Clare; it is lecture-style and by a scholar of Clare. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Clare-of-Assisi-Her-Life-and-Theology-Audiobook/B0786PGX65)
* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Catherine M. Mooney — *Clare of Assisi and the Thirteenth-Century Church: Religious Women, Rules, and Resistance*** — strongest critical historical work I found for Clare; better than most audio options if you want serious scholarship. — [Barnes & Noble](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/clare-of-assisi-and-the-thirteenth-century-church-catherine-m-mooney/1123830991)
* [ ] **P3 / Audible** — **Bret Thoman — *St. Clare of Assisi: Light from the Cloister*** — fuller than a short devotional title, but it includes imaginative reconstruction, so I would rank it below Mueller and Mooney. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/author/Bret-Thoman/B0753K2PTJ)

## Saint Elizabeth of the Trinity

* [ ] **P2 / Audible** — **Julie Enzler, Fr. Ignatius John Schweitzer, and Anthony Lilles — *Named for Glory: St. Elizabeth of the Trinity’s Identity and Mission*** — best substantial Audible item I found; more spiritual-theological than critical biography, but still relevant. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Named-for-Glory-Audiobook/B0FYJ4HBT8)
* [ ] **P2 / Audible** — **Claire Dwyer — *This Present Paradise*** — accessible spiritual biography / guide to Elizabeth; likely more devotional than academic. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/This-Present-Paradise-Audiobook/B0BZ9T6ZVN)
* [ ] **P2 / print-primary** — **Elizabeth of the Trinity — *Complete Works*, Vol. 1, with Conrad De Meester material** — best route for serious primary-source engagement with scholarly framing. — [Sisters of Carmel](https://www.sistersofcarmel.com/elizabeth-of-the-trinity-the-complete-works-volume-one.php)
* [ ] **P3 / print-watchlist** — **Conrad De Meester — *Élisabeth de la Trinité: biographie*** — major biography, but French and not an Audible option found in this pass. — [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL19539388M/Elisabeth_de_la_Trinit%C3%A9)

## Saint Bernadette Soubirous

* [ ] **P1 / print or non-Audible audio-watchlist** — **Ruth Harris — *Lourdes: Body and Spirit in the Secular Age*** — best critical historical context for Bernadette and Lourdes; it treats Lourdes through religion, medicine, science, politics, and modernity. — [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Lourdes-Body-Spirit-Secular-Age/dp/0141038489)
* [ ] **P3 / Audible** — **Diane Allen — *They Walked with God, Book I*** — includes Bernadette along with John Vianney, Damien of Molokai, André Bessette, and Solanus Casey; useful, but not a dedicated critical Bernadette biography. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/series/They-Walked-with-God-Audiobooks/B0DWN83CLC)
* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **Anna Garavitt — *Lourdes: The Miraculous Life of St. Bernadette Soubirous*** — Virtual Voice and miracle-forward; keep only as a low-priority devotional supplement. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Lourdes-The-Miraculous-Life-of-St-Bernadette-Soubirous-and-the-Message-of-Hope-of-Our-Lady-of-Lourdes-Audiobook/B0FGWZLV3C)
* [ ] **P4 / French Audible** — **Père Marie-Antoine de Lavaur — *Bernadette Soubirous*** — French-language option; older/religious rather than modern critical history. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/search?searchProvider=Saint-L%C3%A9ger+productions)

## Saint Rose of Lima

* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Stephen M. Hart — *The Life of St Rose of Lima (1586–1617)*** — strongest critical option found; publisher description says it uses early sources and Vatican archival material and aims to replace guesswork with historical evidence. — [Boydell & Brewer](https://boydellandbrewer.com/book/the-life-of-st-rose-of-lima-1586-1617-pdf/)
* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **Bob and Penny Lord — *Saint Rose of Lima*** — available on Audible, but devotional rather than critical. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Saint-Rose-of-Lima-Audiobook/B08NC7H5VN)
* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **Mary Fabyan Windeatt — *St. Rose of Lima*** — children’s / devotional style; not suited to your historical-critical target. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/St-Rose-of-Lima-Audiobook/B0DKY8FKWX)
* [ ] **P4 / free audio** — **Florence Mary Capes — *St. Rose of Lima: The Flower of the New World*** — older public-domain hagiography; useful only for reception/devotional tradition. — [LibriVox](https://librivox.org/st-rose-of-lima-the-flower-of-the-new-world-by-florence-mary-capes/)

## Saint Margaret of Scotland

* [ ] **P2 / Audible** — **Laurel A. Rockefeller — *Margaret of Wessex*** — closest Audible biography-like option I found for Margaret, but treat it as popular rather than academic. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Margaret-of-Wessex-Audiobook/B0CM7JCHKL)
* [ ] **P2 / podcast-audio** — **“Scotland’s Medieval Queens” episode with historian Sharon Bennett Connolly** — useful historical context for Margaret and medieval Scottish queenship. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/podcast/Scotlands-Medieval-Queens/B0GQFMZFDZ)
* [ ] **P3 / primary-source print** — **Turgot — *Life of St. Margaret, Queen of Scotland*** — important medieval source, but not modern critical biography. — [Barnes & Noble](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/life-of-st-margaret-queen-of-scotland-turgot/1102129920)
* [ ] **P4 / free audio** — ***The Sainted Queens*** — includes Margaret, but is older/devotional rather than historical-critical. — [LibriVox](https://librivox.org/the-sainted-queens-by-unknown/)

## Saint Teresa of Ávila

* [ ] **P1 / Audible lecture** — **Elizabeth A. Dreyer — *Great Catholic Women: Teresa of Ávila, Catherine of Siena, Thérèse of Lisieux, Hildegard of Bingen*** — best lecture-style Audible addition for Teresa; also useful for three other major women saints. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Great-Catholic-Women-Audiobook/B074G57DZT)
* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Cathleen Medwick — *Teresa of Avila: The Progress of a Soul*** — strong modern biography; I did not find it as a current Audible title. — [Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/112873/teresa-of-avila-by-cathleen-medwick/)
* [ ] **P2 / audiobook-primary** — **Teresa of Ávila — *The Book of My Life*** — primary spiritual autobiography; not critical biography, but essential for Teresa. — [Shambhala](https://www.shambhala.com/teresa-of-avila-2871.html)
* [ ] **P2 / Audible lecture-context** — ***Introduction to Christian Mysticism*** — includes Teresa of Ávila, John of the Cross, Catherine of Siena, Julian of Norwich, and others; good context, not a dedicated biography. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/Introduction-to-Christian-Mysticism-Audiobook/B0743NLPJ7)
* [ ] **P3 / podcast-lecture** — **Discerning Hearts — *The Life of St. Teresa of Avila*** — useful guided overview, but confessional rather than historical-critical. — [Discerning Hearts](https://www.discerninghearts.com/catholic-podcasts/btp-lot1-introduction-the-life-of-st-teresa-of-avila-beginning-to-pray-with-dr-anthony-lilles-podcast/)

## Saint Jerome

## Saint Thomas Aquinas

# 2. Thin historical-data saints: keep low priority

## Saint Lucy of Syracuse

* [ ] **P1 / print-reception history** — **Karen A. Winstead — *Chaste Passions: Medieval English Virgin Martyr Legends*** — best serious scholarly item for Lucy-type material; it is about medieval virgin martyr legends rather than recoverable biography. — [Cornell University Press](https://www.cornellpress.cornell.edu/book/9780801435690/chaste-passions/)
* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **Wyatt North — *The Life and Prayers of Saint Lucy of Syracuse*** — Virtual Voice and explicitly part biography / part prayer book; not historical-critical. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Life-and-Prayers-of-Saint-Lucy-of-Syracuse-Audiobook/B0FXF56LMZ)

## Saint Anastasia

* [ ] **P1 / print-reception history** — **Karen A. Winstead — *Chaste Passions*** — best serious route for Anastasia as a virgin-martyr legend; useful for literary and hagiographical reception, not secure biography. — [Cornell University Press](https://www.cornellpress.cornell.edu/book/9780801435690/chaste-passions/)
* [ ] **P3 / Orthodox-liturgical primary** — **Canons of Saint Anastasia of Serbia** — relevant only for **St. Anastasia of Serbia**, not the Roman/Sirmian martyr Anastasia. Treat as liturgical source, not biography. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/St_Anastasia_of_Serbia_Canons_of_Saint_Anastasia_o?id=JXUcEQAAQBAJ)
* [ ] **P4 / note** — I did not find a strong Audible critical biography for Anastasia of Sirmium/Rome. The historically useful path is martyr-legend scholarship, not Audible biography.

# 3. Ambiguous name: “Saint Richard the Great”

I did not find a standard saint commonly called **“Saint Richard the Great.”** The likely possibilities are below.

* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **“Day by Day”** — uses the prayer associated with St. Richard of Chichester, but it is a short prayer/music track, not a biography. — [Audible](https://www.audible.com/search?searchAuthor=Frederic+Chopin)
* [ ] **P3 / print-source watchlist** — **Saint Richard of Chichester: The Sources for His Life** — better than the Audible prayer track, but still source material rather than a modern audiobook biography. — [West Sussex Libraries](https://libraries.westsussex.gov.uk/Author/Home?author=%22Jones%2C+David%22&basicSearchType=Author&sort=popularity+desc&view=list)
