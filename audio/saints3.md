## Named-saint Audible / audiobook / lecture pass

**Priority key:**
**P1** = best historical-critical option, even when print-only
**P2** = useful serious audio, lecture, primary source, or sympathetic biography
**P3** = decent supplement but not strongly critical
**P4** = devotional, children’s, miracle-forward, Virtual Voice, or very thin historically

---

# 1. Best additions for your list

## Saint Helena

* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Julia Hillner — *Helena Augusta: Mother of the Empire*** — best critical historical option I found for Helena; not an Audible title in this pass. It treats Helena in Constantinian dynastic, imperial, gender, and political context rather than as simple legend. ([barnesandnoble.com][1])
* [ ] **P3 / Audible** — **Steve Castlen — *St. Helena: The Most Significant Woman in Human History*** — closest Audible biography, but the description frames it as a blend of historical fact and imaginative storytelling, so it is not ideal for strict historical-critical purposes. ([Audible.com][2])
* [ ] **P3 / Audible podcast** — **“St. Helena and the Finding of the True Cross”** — short lecture/podcast-style supplement, useful only as a quick overview. ([Audible.com][3])

## Saint Clare of Assisi

* [ ] **P1 / Audible lecture** — **Joan Mueller — *Clare of Assisi: Her Life and Theology*** — still the best Audible option for Clare; it is lecture-style and by a scholar of Clare. ([Audible.com][4])
* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Catherine M. Mooney — *Clare of Assisi and the Thirteenth-Century Church: Religious Women, Rules, and Resistance*** — strongest critical historical work I found for Clare; better than most audio options if you want serious scholarship. ([barnesandnoble.com][5])
* [ ] **P3 / Audible** — **Bret Thoman — *St. Clare of Assisi: Light from the Cloister*** — fuller than a short devotional title, but it includes imaginative reconstruction, so I would rank it below Mueller and Mooney. ([Audible.com][6])

## Saint Elizabeth of the Trinity

* [ ] **P2 / Audible** — **Julie Enzler, Fr. Ignatius John Schweitzer, and Anthony Lilles — *Named for Glory: St. Elizabeth of the Trinity’s Identity and Mission*** — best substantial Audible item I found; more spiritual-theological than critical biography, but still relevant. ([Audible.com][7])
* [ ] **P2 / Audible** — **Claire Dwyer — *This Present Paradise*** — accessible spiritual biography / guide to Elizabeth; likely more devotional than academic. ([Audible.com][8])
* [ ] **P2 / print-primary** — **Elizabeth of the Trinity — *Complete Works*, Vol. 1, with Conrad De Meester material** — best route for serious primary-source engagement with scholarly framing. ([sistersofcarmel.com][9])
* [ ] **P3 / print-watchlist** — **Conrad De Meester — *Élisabeth de la Trinité: biographie*** — major biography, but French and not an Audible option found in this pass. ([Open Library][10])

## Saint Bernadette Soubirous

* [ ] **P1 / print or non-Audible audio-watchlist** — **Ruth Harris — *Lourdes: Body and Spirit in the Secular Age*** — best critical historical context for Bernadette and Lourdes; it treats Lourdes through religion, medicine, science, politics, and modernity. ([Amazon][11])
* [ ] **P3 / Audible** — **Diane Allen — *They Walked with God, Book I*** — includes Bernadette along with John Vianney, Damien of Molokai, André Bessette, and Solanus Casey; useful, but not a dedicated critical Bernadette biography. ([Audible.com][12])
* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **Anna Garavitt — *Lourdes: The Miraculous Life of St. Bernadette Soubirous*** — Virtual Voice and miracle-forward; keep only as a low-priority devotional supplement. ([Audible.com][13])
* [ ] **P4 / French Audible** — **Père Marie-Antoine de Lavaur — *Bernadette Soubirous*** — French-language option; older/religious rather than modern critical history. ([Audible.com][14])

## Saint Rose of Lima

* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Stephen M. Hart — *The Life of St Rose of Lima (1586–1617)*** — strongest critical option found; publisher description says it uses early sources and Vatican archival material and aims to replace guesswork with historical evidence. ([Boydell and Brewer][15])
* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **Bob and Penny Lord — *Saint Rose of Lima*** — available on Audible, but devotional rather than critical. ([Audible.com][16])
* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **Mary Fabyan Windeatt — *St. Rose of Lima*** — children’s / devotional style; not suited to your historical-critical target. ([Audible.com][17])
* [ ] **P4 / free audio** — **Florence Mary Capes — *St. Rose of Lima: The Flower of the New World*** — older public-domain hagiography; useful only for reception/devotional tradition. ([LibriVox][18])

## Saint Margaret of Scotland

* [ ] **P2 / Audible** — **Laurel A. Rockefeller — *Margaret of Wessex*** — closest Audible biography-like option I found for Margaret, but treat it as popular rather than academic. ([Audible.com][19])
* [ ] **P2 / podcast-audio** — **“Scotland’s Medieval Queens” episode with historian Sharon Bennett Connolly** — useful historical context for Margaret and medieval Scottish queenship. ([Audible.com][20])
* [ ] **P3 / primary-source print** — **Turgot — *Life of St. Margaret, Queen of Scotland*** — important medieval source, but not modern critical biography. ([barnesandnoble.com][21])
* [ ] **P4 / free audio** — ***The Sainted Queens*** — includes Margaret, but is older/devotional rather than historical-critical. ([LibriVox][22])

## Saint Teresa of Ávila

* [ ] **P1 / Audible lecture** — **Elizabeth A. Dreyer — *Great Catholic Women: Teresa of Ávila, Catherine of Siena, Thérèse of Lisieux, Hildegard of Bingen*** — best lecture-style Audible addition for Teresa; also useful for three other major women saints. ([Audible.com][23])
* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Cathleen Medwick — *Teresa of Avila: The Progress of a Soul*** — strong modern biography; I did not find it as a current Audible title. ([PenguinRandomhouse.com][24])
* [ ] **P2 / audiobook-primary** — **Teresa of Ávila — *The Book of My Life*** — primary spiritual autobiography; not critical biography, but essential for Teresa. ([Shambhala Pubs][25])
* [ ] **P2 / Audible lecture-context** — ***Introduction to Christian Mysticism*** — includes Teresa of Ávila, John of the Cross, Catherine of Siena, Julian of Norwich, and others; good context, not a dedicated biography. ([Audible.com][26])
* [ ] **P3 / podcast-lecture** — **Discerning Hearts — *The Life of St. Teresa of Avila*** — useful guided overview, but confessional rather than historical-critical. ([Discerning Hearts Catholic Podcasts][27])

## Saint Henry II, Holy Roman Emperor

* [ ] **P2 / free audio** — ***The Cambridge Medieval History, Vol. 3: Germany and the Western Empire*** — includes multiple sections on **Emperor Henry II**; probably the closest serious audio history for St. Henry II. ([LibriVox][28])
* [ ] **P2 / primary-source print** — **Adalbold II of Utrecht — *On the Deeds of St. Henry*** — primary source on Henry II; useful, but not modern critical biography. ([barnesandnoble.com][29])
* [ ] **P3 / Audible context** — **Peter H. Wilson — *Heart of Europe: A History of the Holy Roman Empire*** — not a Henry biography, but probably the better Audible route for imperial context. ([Amazon][30])
* [ ] **P4 / Audible context** — **James Bryce — *The Holy Roman Empire*** — classic older context; useful, but dated. ([Audible.com][31])

## Saint John Henry Newman

* [ ] **P1 / Audible** — **Roderick Strange — *John Henry Newman: A Mind Alive*** — best dedicated Newman biography-like Audible option I found. ([Audible.com][32])
* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Ian Ker — *John Henry Newman: A Biography*** — major full biography; I did not find a current Audible edition. ([Amazon][33])
* [ ] **P2 / Audible lecture** — **Philip G. Bochanski — *John Henry Newman: Faith, Letters, Legacy*** — lecture-style Catholic overview, useful after or alongside Strange. ([Audible.com][34])
* [ ] **P2 / Audible primary** — **John Henry Newman — *Apologia Pro Vita Sua*** — already on your list, but still essential as Newman’s own intellectual and religious self-defense. ([Audible.com][35])

## Saint Jerome

* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **J. N. D. Kelly — *Jerome: His Life, Writings, and Controversies*** — best serious biography found; I did not find a current Audible edition. ([Amazon][36])
* [ ] **P2 / free audio-primary** — **Jerome — *On Illustrious Men*** — primary source from Jerome, not a biography of Jerome. Useful for his intellectual world. ([Internet Archive][37])
* [ ] **P3 / podcast-lecture** — **Way of the Fathers episode on Jerome** — useful short contextual supplement, not a critical biography. ([Way of the Fathers][38])

## Saint Thomas Aquinas

* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Jean-Pierre Torrell — *Saint Thomas Aquinas: The Person and His Work*** — strongest scholarly biography/intellectual history option found; not an Audible title. ([Catholic University of America Press][39])
* [ ] **P1 / print-watchlist** — **Denys Turner — *Thomas Aquinas: A Portrait*** — shorter critical/intellectual portrait; useful if Torrell is too large. ([Google Play][40])
* [ ] **P2 / Audible** — **Edward Feser — *Aquinas*** — best Audible intellectual introduction found; more philosophical than biographical. ([Audible.com][41])
* [ ] **P2 / Audible context** — **Richard Woods — *St. Dominic and the Dominican Way*** — gives Dominican institutional context for Aquinas, Albert the Great, and later Dominican theologians. ([Audible.com][42])
* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **G. K. Chesterton — *Saint Thomas Aquinas*** — classic Catholic literary portrait, but not modern critical history. ([Audible.com][43])
* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **Hourly History — *Thomas Aquinas: A Life from Beginning to End*** — very short overview; use only as a quick orientation. ([Audible.com][44])

# 2. Thin historical-data saints: keep low priority

## Saint Lucy of Syracuse

* [ ] **P1 / print-reception history** — **Karen A. Winstead — *Chaste Passions: Medieval English Virgin Martyr Legends*** — best serious scholarly item for Lucy-type material; it is about medieval virgin martyr legends rather than recoverable biography. ([Cornell University Press][45])
* [ ] **P4 / Audible** — **Wyatt North — *The Life and Prayers of Saint Lucy of Syracuse*** — Virtual Voice and explicitly part biography / part prayer book; not historical-critical. ([Audible.com][46])

## Saint Anastasia

* [ ] **P1 / print-reception history** — **Karen A. Winstead — *Chaste Passions*** — best serious route for Anastasia as a virgin-martyr legend; useful for literary and hagiographical reception, not secure biography. ([Cornell University Press][45])
* [ ] **P3 / Orthodox-liturgical primary** — **Canons of Saint Anastasia of Serbia** — relevant only for **St. Anastasia of Serbia**, not the Roman/Sirmian martyr Anastasia. Treat as liturgical source, not biography. ([Google Play][47])
* [ ] **P4 / note** — I did not find a strong Audible critical biography for Anastasia of Sirmium/Rome. The historically useful path is martyr-legend scholarship, not Audible biography.
